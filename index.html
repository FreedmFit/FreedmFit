About Nova

As a dedicated weight loss coach and personal trainer, I've made it my mission to help busy individuals, particularly men, achieve their fitness goals without sacrificing their lifestyle.

My journey began when I realized that traditional fitness approaches often fail because they don't account for real-life constraints. I've developed a sustainable system that works with your schedule, not against it.

With certifications in personal training and nutritional coaching, I bring both expertise and personal experience to help you transform not just your body, but your relationship with fitness.
